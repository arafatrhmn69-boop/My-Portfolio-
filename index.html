The idea

Every place has a story. Every journey becomes a memory.

This is not a resume with a few photographs attached. It is a personal archive — a place for coastlines, hill roads, quiet evenings and the small details that stay after a trip ends.

I am also learning to build for the web. HTML, CSS, Bootstrap and JavaScript are the tools I am practising, while photography gives me a reason to pay attention. This site brings those two parts of my life together: the things I explore and the things I am learning to make.

03 / About me

Still learning.
Always exploring.

I'm a student and an aspiring web developer who feels most curious when there is a new place ahead.

I'm interested in technology and patiently learning how websites are built — from semantic HTML and thoughtful CSS to responsive layouts and JavaScript interactions. I'm not a senior developer; I'm a learner who enjoys making small things work and understanding why.

Outside the screen, I'm drawn to natural landscapes, cultural places and the in-between moments of a journey. I like photography because it asks me to slow down, notice light, and document a place honestly.
