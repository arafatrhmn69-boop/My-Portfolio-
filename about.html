Hello, I'm Arafat Rahman

Two kinds of curiosity.

One makes me open a code editor. The other makes me pack a bag.

As a student, I am learning web development step by step. I'm interested in technology because it lets an idea become something another person can open, read and experience. Right now that means practising semantic HTML, expressive CSS, responsive layouts with Bootstrap and Vanilla JavaScript.

I'm an aspiring developer, not an expert. This website is one of my real practice projects: a place to make mistakes, understand the code and slowly improve the way a story is presented on a screen.

Travelling is the other half of the picture. I'm drawn to natural landscapes, local culture, coastlines, hills, village roads and the small unplanned pauses between destinations. Photography helps me document those moments and remember how a place felt — not just how it looked.

The Journey Archive is where both interests meet: a personal record of places and a project I can keep building as my skills grow.
